## The pattern we keep seeing

Most martech stacks aren't over-bought on purpose. They're the accumulated result of a dozen reasonable decisions made in isolation — a point solution here, a "temporary" integration there — until nobody can draw the full picture from memory anymore.

## What to check before adding the next tool

A few questions worth asking before any new line item hits the budget: does this overlap with something we already own, who actually owns the outcome it's meant to improve, and what happens to the data if we ever have to unwind it.

## The takeaway

None of this requires a full replatform. It just requires someone willing to ask the boring questions before the exciting purchase.
