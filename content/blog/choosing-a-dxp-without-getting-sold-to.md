## The demo is not the product

A vendor demo is built to show the platform at its absolute best, on a dataset that was never going to expose the seams. That's not dishonest — it's just not the question you're trying to answer.

## Questions worth asking instead

Ask what the last three customers who churned had in common. Ask to speak to someone who's had the platform for three years, not three months. Ask what breaks first when the team that championed the purchase moves on.

## The takeaway

The best DXP for your organisation is rarely the one with the flashiest demo — it's the one that still makes sense two reorgs and one budget cycle later.
